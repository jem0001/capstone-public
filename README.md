# Teacher-Led Multimodal Web Learning Application

A web-based educational platform designed for **elementary students** of **Sta. Filomena Integrated School**, focused on **Araling Panlipunan**. This application integrates **games**, **3D polarized videos**, and **QR-based student response** to support interactive and teacher-guided learning on a **single device**.

## Objective

To enhance engagement and understanding of Araling Panlipunan topics through multimodal learning techniques, combining traditional instruction with interactive digital tools.

## Key Features

- **Teacher-Only Account System**  
  Only teachers log in and manage content, student records, and activities.

- **Gamified Learning Modules**  
  Educational games aligned with the curriculum that reward students with points.

- **3D Video Integration**  
  Videos viewed through improvised 3D glasses for an immersive learning experience.

- **QR Code-Based Student Identification**  
  Each student is assigned a unique QR code to scan after completing an activity.

- **Points Tracking and Activity History**  
  Points earned are recorded along with specific activities and organized by academic quarter.

## How It Works

1. Teacher logs into the platform.
2. Teacher selects a week/topic and either plays a 3D video or launches a game.
3. Students take turns responding to gamified questions.
4. After completing, they scan their QR code to log their points.
5. Points are stored and displayed in their activity record for monitoring.
